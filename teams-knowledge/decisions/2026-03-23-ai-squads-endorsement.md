# Endorse AI Squads as Future Direction

## Decision

The AI Squads program is endorsed as the future direction for squad-based AI workflows. Team members should join the AI Squads walkthrough to understand the new architecture and capabilities.

## Rationale

- AI Squads represents a significant evolution in how agent-based work is orchestrated
- Early adoption positions the team ahead of broader organizational adoption
- Walkthrough provides hands-on understanding of new patterns and tooling
- Alignment with organizational direction reduces future refactoring risk

## Implications

- Squad structure and workflows may need to adapt to AI Squads patterns
- Existing worktree-based orchestration (current approach) may be replaced or supplemented by AI Squads approach
- Team should prioritize attending the walkthrough to build shared understanding
